# Google Ads Campaign Plan: Campaign 2 — Home Accessories

## 1. Campaign Overview & Objective
- **Campaign Name:** `HH_Search_HomeAccessories_India`
- **Marketing Objective:** **Sales & Category Engagement** (Multi-item basket building across tabletop decor, vases, cushions, and candles).
- **Campaign Type:** Google Search Network.
- **Target Geographic Location:** India (Metro & Tier 1/2 Urban Centers).
- **Languages:** English.
- **Proposed Daily Budget:** ₹450.00 / day.
- **Destination Landing Page:** `/collections/home-accessories` (Live Route: `https://haven-and-hue.netlify.app/collections/home-accessories`).

---

## 2. Target Audience Profile
- **Demographics:** Adults aged 20–35, urban apartment dwellers, interior design enthusiasts.
- **Interests / Affinity Segments:** Home & Garden, Coffee Table Decor, Candles & Fragrance, Cozy Home Decor, Minimalist Living.
- **Search Behavior:** Customers looking for decorative accents (ceramic donut vases, soy candles, bouclé cushions) to style their shelves, console tables, and living room corners.

- **Bidding Strategy:** **Maximize Clicks with Max CPC Bid Limit (₹10.50)**. Lower average CPC compared to wall art and lighting allows for higher click volume within the ₹450 daily budget.
