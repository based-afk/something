# Google Ads Campaign Plan: Campaign 1 — Wall Art & Decor

## 1. Campaign Overview & Objective
- **Campaign Name:** `HH_Search_WallArt_Decor_India`
- **Marketing Objective:** **Sales & Website Traffic** (Commercial intent conversion to product detail engagement and simulated checkouts).
- **Campaign Type:** Google Search Network (Search Partners opted in, Display Network excluded for higher intent).
- **Target Geographic Location:** India (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Metros: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata).
- **Languages:** English.
- **Proposed Daily Budget:** ₹500.00 / day.
- **Destination Landing Page:** `/collections/wall-art` (Live Route: `https://haven-and-hue.netlify.app/collections/wall-art`).

---

## 2. Target Audience Profile
- **Demographics:** Men & Women, aged 22–35.
- **Interests / Affinity Segments:** Interior Decor Enthusiasts, Home Improvement, New Home Buyers, Minimalist Lifestyle, Art & Design Lovers.
- **Search Behavior:** Young professionals seeking ready-to-hang, tasteful art pieces to decorate newly rented apartments or first homes without excessive custom framing expenses.

## 6. Bidding Strategy & Budget
- **Bidding Strategy:** **Maximize Clicks with Max CPC Bid Limit (₹15.00)** during initial learning days, progressing to **Target CPA (₹95.00)** or **Maximize Conversions** once conversion history accumulates.
- **Ad Schedule:** All days, 08:00 AM – 11:30 PM (peak browsing & evening purchase window).

## 7. Ad Placement & Ad Rank Explanation
- **Ad Placement:** Top of Google Search Results Page (Top of Page / Absolute Top).
- **Ad Rank Mechanics:**
  $$\text{Ad Rank} = f(\text{Max CPC Bid}, \text{Quality Score}, \text{Ad Extensions Impact})$$
  - **Expected CTR:** High due to exact title matching and clear price callout (₹799).

  - **Landing Page Experience:** The dedicated `/collections/wall-art` landing page loads instantly with matching hero headline, product specifications, and direct product detail links.
